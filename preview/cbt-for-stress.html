Recognising stress

What does stress feel like?

You may find yourself feeling overwhelmed, irritable, exhausted, unable to relax or constantly worrying about what needs to be done next. If stress is taking over your life, CBT can help.

Stress affects everyone differently, but common signs include:

  • Constant worry
  • Feeling overwhelmed
  • Racing thoughts
  • Difficulty switching off
  • Trouble sleeping
  • Feeling irritable or emotional
  • Muscle tension
  • Headaches
  • Low motivation
  • Exhausted even after resting

Many people continue pushing through stress without realising the impact it is having on their wellbeing.

When stress becomes too much

Stress can develop gradually. You may have become so used to feeling busy and overwhelmed that it now feels normal.

Over time, prolonged stress can lead to anxiety, burnout and reduced confidence.

You might find yourself:

  • Constantly thinking about work
  • Struggling to relax at weekends
  • Feeling guilty when resting
  • Overthinking decisions
  • Becoming impatient with loved ones
  • Feeling emotionally drained

How CBT can help with stress

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is a practical, evidence-based approach that helps you understand the connection between your thoughts, feelings and behaviours.

Rather than simply managing symptoms, CBT helps address the patterns that keep stress going.

  • Manage stress more effectively
  • Reduce excessive worry
  • Learn healthy coping strategies
  • Improve work-life balance
  • Challenge unhelpful thinking patterns
  • Build resilience and confidence
  • Prevent burnout

Stress, anxiety and burnout

Many people are unsure whether they are experiencing stress, anxiety or burnout. Although these experiences can overlap, understanding the difference can be helpful.

Stress

Often linked to external pressures such as work, family responsibilities, financial concerns or major life changes.

Anxiety

May continue even when the stressful situation has passed.

Burnout

Often develops after prolonged periods of stress and can leave you feeling emotionally exhausted and disconnected.

Together we can explore what is contributing to your difficulties and develop a plan that supports your wellbeing.
