Frequently asked questions

The questions I’m most often asked by clients and people thinking about counselling, including fees and how appointments work.

Fees & appointments

Can’t find your answer? Get in touch and I’ll be happy to help.

Ask a question
How much does it cost?
Please contact me for fees on 083 854 1298 or bredamorrisseycounsellor@gmail.com.
How long does each appointment last?
50 to 60 minutes.
How do I schedule an appointment?
Email bredamorrisseycounsellor@gmail.com, or text, WhatsApp or ring 083 854 1298. You can also DM @BredaMorrisseyCBT.
How often should I come to therapy?
Depending on the issues you want to address, I will help you decide how many sessions are appropriate. This is something we can look at in the first session and review regularly.
What is the cancellation policy?
A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required, otherwise the full fee will be charged.
How do I pay?
Payments are made by bank transfer or PayPal.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Thursday, 7am to 7pm. Friday, 7am to 6pm.

Ready to take the next step?

If you’re feeling ready, I’d be very happy to hear from you. Get in touch to ask a question or arrange an initial appointment. You don’t have to have everything figured out. Just starting the conversation is enough.
