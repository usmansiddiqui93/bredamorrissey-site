Whatever brought you here, you don’t have to keep carrying it alone

Do you find yourself overthinking everything, constantly worrying, or feeling stuck in your own mind? Maybe your confidence has taken a knock, or you’re carrying experiences that have left you feeling like you’re not quite good enough.

I’m Breda Morrissey, an accredited counsellor and psychotherapist specialising in CBT, counselling and mindfulness. I provide warm, confidential online counselling for adults across Ireland, helping you understand what you’re experiencing, develop practical coping strategies and feel more confident in yourself and your life.

All from the comfort of your own home.

How I can help

Support for the things that weigh on you

I support people who are struggling with:

  • Anxiety and constant worry
  • Overthinking and racing thoughts
  • Low self-esteem and lack of confidence
  • Feeling stuck, lost, or unsure about life
  • Emotional impact of illness, including cancer
  • Fertility challenges, including IVF and loss
  • Stress, overwhelm, and burnout

You might not have the “right words” for how you feel, and that’s okay. We can figure it out together.

A safe, supportive space to talk

I offer a calm, confidential space where you can talk openly, feel heard, and begin to make sense of what’s going on for you.

Together, we can gently explore what’s been weighing on you and help you find ways to feel more in control, more confident, and more like yourself again.

How we’ll work together

I draw on a combination of counselling and Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), always tailored to you as an individual. There’s no one-size-fits-all approach. Our work together will move at a pace that feels right for you.

This is your space to talk freely, without judgement or pressure.

What to expect

Starting therapy can feel like a big step, especially if you’ve never done it before.

In our first session, we’ll take things gently. We’ll talk about what’s been going on for you, what you’d like support with, and answer any questions you may have.

There’s no pressure, just a chance to begin.
