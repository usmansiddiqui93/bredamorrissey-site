About me

Hi, I’m Breda

Reaching out for counselling isn’t easy. You might be used to putting your needs last. Maybe you’re telling yourself that you should be able to cope.

I always tell my clients that seeking support isn’t a sign of weakness; it’s the beginning of taking back control over your life. Because when you have the confidence to approach challenges and the coping tools to navigate life experiences, the positive effects span far beyond your mental health.

With a passion for helping people regain their confidence, my goal is for you to leave therapy feeling energised and excited for the future.

But more than anything, I want you to feel comfortable being yourself. You don’t need to pretend everything is fine, have the right words, or know exactly what you want from therapy.

We’ll work that out together.

Getting started

How we’ll work together

Taking the first step is the hardest part of therapy, but I’m here to make everything easier.

First, we’ll have a no-obligation chat: a chance for me to learn what’s important to you, and for you to learn how I can help you move forward. Each session is tailored to you, because therapy isn’t just about approaches and exercises. It’s a space we create, where you feel able to share, learn, and reclaim your power.

  1. 01Step 1

    Our first conversation

    Our first conversation is an opportunity to get to know each other. I’ll ask a little about what’s brought you to counselling, what you’re hoping to achieve and how you’ve been feeling.

    You can ask questions too. It’s important that you feel comfortable with the person you’re talking to. There’s no obligation to proceed with therapy.

    No obligation
  2. 02Step 2

    Your online counselling sessions

    We’ll meet online, so you can attend sessions from somewhere private and comfortable without having to travel.

    Sessions can include exploring difficult experiences, understanding your feelings, and developing practical ways to manage everyday changes that work for you.

    Everything is on your terms. I don’t expect you to share anything before you’re ready.

    From your own home
  3. 03Step 3

    Moving forward at your own pace

    There’s no fixed timeline for feeling better. Some people come to counselling with a specific concern, while others need more time to understand what they’re experiencing.

    We’ll regularly check in on how you’re feeling and whether our sessions are helping you move towards your goals.

    Progress reviewed together

Where do you offer online counselling services?−

I provide confidential online counselling for adults across Ireland and the UK. Whether you’re based in Dublin, Cork, Limerick, Galway, Tipperary or elsewhere, you can access support without travelling to an appointment.

All you need is a reliable internet connection and a private, comfortable space where you feel able to talk freely. I’ll confirm that online counselling is suitable and that I can provide services in your location before we begin.

How much does online counselling cost?+

Please get in touch for current fees. Sessions last 50 to 60 minutes. You can also arrange an initial consultation to ask questions, discuss what you’re looking for and decide whether you’d feel comfortable working with me.

I’ll explain the fees and booking arrangements before your first appointment, so there are no unexpected costs.

In-person vs online therapy: which is better for me?+

Both online and in-person counselling can be effective. The right choice depends on your preferences, circumstances and the type of support you need. We can discuss whether online counselling is a suitable option for you during our initial conversation.

How many counselling sessions will I need?+

There’s no set number of counselling sessions that works for everyone. We’ll discuss your goals and review your progress together, so you can make informed decisions about continuing therapy.

Who is CBT not suitable for?+

CBT can be effective for many difficulties, including anxiety and depression, but it isn’t necessarily the right approach for everyone. I’ll discuss your circumstances with you and, if another type of support would be more appropriate, help you consider your options.

Can I talk to a therapist online about more than one issue?+

Absolutely. It’s common for anxiety, low confidence, relationship difficulties and other emotional challenges to overlap. We’ll explore what’s happening in your life and focus on the areas that matter most to you.

Is online counselling confidential?+

Yes. Confidentiality is an important part of counselling. Before we begin, I’ll explain how your information is handled and the limited circumstances in which confidentiality may need to be broken, such as serious safety concerns.
